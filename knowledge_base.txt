[Globex Corp - Internal Policies]

Remote Work Policy:
Employees may work remotely up to two days a week. Core hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the local time zone, during which employees must be available for meetings.

International Travel Policy:
Employees may travel internationally for business if approved by their department head at least 30 days in advance. All flights must be booked in economy class unless the duration exceeds 8 hours, permitting business class. The company covers meals up to $75 per day. 

Pet Policy (Office):
Well-behaved cats and dogs are allowed in the office on Wednesdays and Fridays. Pets must be vaccinated and kept on a leash or in a carrier at all times.
